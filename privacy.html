Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy covers "Loot Beam Uploader", a private application operated by Loot Beam Clips.

What the app does

The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload and manage videos on the Loot Beam Clips YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner, for the channel owner's own account.

Data we access

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the Loot Beam Clips YouTube account to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags, and schedules. It does not access, collect, or store data about any other users.

Storage and sharing

Authorization tokens are stored securely on the owner's private computer and are used only to operate the channel. We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions.
